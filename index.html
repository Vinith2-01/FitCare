Beginner Guide

How to Start Working Out (When You Haven’t in Years)

The secret is momentum over intensity. Don't start with a grueling 90-minute gym session. Start with a 10-minute daily walk or 5 gentle bodyweight squats while waiting for your kettle to boil.

  • Consistency > Perfection: 15 minutes 4 times a week beats 2 hours once every 3 weeks.
  • Find joy in movement: Dance, hike, stretch, swim. If you hate running, don't run!
  • Celebrate non-scale victories: Energy levels, better mood, improved sleep, lifting heavier groceries.
Mindset Shift

The 80/20 Rule for Sustainable Health

Extreme diets fail because humans are not robots. Life includes birthday cakes, cozy pizza nights with family, and spontaneous ice cream with friends.

  • 80% Nourish: Wholesome, unprocessed foods, colorful plants, lean proteins, high hydration, good rest.
  • 20% Enjoy: Your favorite treats, comfort meals, and social dining with zero guilt.
  • No "Cheat Days": Remove moral labels from food. Food is not "good" or "bad"—you are not "good" or "bad" for eating it.
